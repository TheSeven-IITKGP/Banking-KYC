Responsible AI & Failure Policy

          
    
            
  • Inbound Guard: Prompt injection, jailbreak, XSS/SQL, and out-of-domain filters trigger REJECTED_SECURITY_VIOLATION.
    • 
            
  • Optical Quality Refusal: Blur/glare or OCR confidence below 0.70 returns REJECTED_POOR_QUALITY without guessing.
    • 
            
  • Outbound PII Masking: Aadhaar masked to XXXX-XXXX-NNNN, PAN middle digits masked, mobile masked to +91-XXXXXXNNNN.
    • 
            
  • Discrepancy Escalation: ID, DOB, or name mismatches below threshold route to MANUAL_REVIEW_OR_REJECT.
