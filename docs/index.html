Responsible AI & Failure Policy

Deterministic safety and compliance guardrails enforced around model inference.

  • Mandatory PII Redaction: PIIRedactor masks Aadhaar numbers, PAN IDs, and mobile numbers before outbound inference calls.
  • Grounded RBI Citations: Regulatory Q&A responses cite retrieved RBI Master Direction 2016 paragraphs from the FAISS index.
  • Escalation on Low Confidence: Blurry scans, missing IDs, or name mismatches route to NEEDS_MANUAL_REVIEW rather than guessing.
  • Zero Raw Document Retention: Uploaded document buffers are processed transiently in memory without unencrypted persistence.
